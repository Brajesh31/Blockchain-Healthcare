# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make this project a respectful, inclusive, and careful learning space. Healthcare and blockchain discussions can involve sensitive topics, and responsible disagreement is welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Explain technical feedback with reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy, patient dignity, and intellectual property.
- Avoid sharing personal data, real health information, or credentials.
- Distinguish demonstrated behavior from assumptions.
- Credit sources and contributors accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential disclosure, privacy violations, plagiarism, or encouraging unsafe use of real health data is not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through <https://github.com/brajesh31>. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy.
