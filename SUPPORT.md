# Support

Read [docs/installation.md](docs/installation.md), [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md), and [docs/faq.md](docs/faq.md) first. Include browser, chain ID, contract address, and sanitized console output; never include private keys, seed phrases, or patient data.
